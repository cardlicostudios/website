Privacy

Cardlico is a game, not a data business. Here's exactly what we collect, and everything we deliberately don't.

What we collect

  • An optional username, if you choose to set one for leaderboards
  • Your scores and run stats, so leaderboards and progress tracking work
  • Anonymous, aggregated analytics on gameplay (e.g. which levels get replayed) to guide balancing

What we don't collect

  • No personal data — no real name, no phone number, no contacts
  • No advertising identifiers and no ad tracking of any kind
  • No location data, ever

Third parties

Cardlico uses Supabase to store gameplay data (scores, leaderboards, optional usernames) securely. We don't sell, share, or trade data with advertisers or data brokers. No other third-party services have access to your data.

Your choices

You can play Cardlico entirely offline in Campaign and Endless Mode without ever setting a username. Leaderboard participation is optional, and you can request your data be deleted at any time via the contact below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a data deletion request? Reach out at privacy@cardlico.com (placeholder — update before launch).
