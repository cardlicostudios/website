Privacy Policy

How Cardlico handles information when you play, use online features, or visit this website.

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Cardlico Studios operates Cardlico and cardlico.com. This policy covers the game and this website. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@cardlico.com.

Game data on your device

Cardlico stores your progress, scores, settings, achievements and owned items on your device so you can continue playing. This game save is not a cloud backup. Core offline play does not require connecting a Google account.

Optional online leaderboards

If you choose to connect to online leaderboards using Google, Google and our authentication provider, Supabase, process your sign-in information. This includes an account identifier, email address and profile information provided through sign-in. We do not receive your Google password.

Supabase stores your player profile and eligible leaderboard records, including your display name, score, cards completed and selected difficulty. Your leaderboard display name and results are visible to other players using the online leaderboard. Your email address is not displayed in leaderboard entries.

We use this information to authenticate players, provide rankings and protect online features from abuse. Local progress and owned items are not uploaded as a general cloud save.

Rewarded advertising

Cardlico uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads when available. Google's Mobile Ads SDK can process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location.

Advertising data processing depends on your region, device settings, consent choices and the ad service's configuration. Choosing non-personalized ads does not necessarily stop all data processing needed to deliver ads or prevent fraud.

For Google's practices, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps and sites.

Advertising privacy choices

Where required and available, Google's consent form lets you make advertising privacy choices. You can revisit those choices through the advertising privacy control in the game's Settings, under Privacy & Data, when that control is available. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings.

You can choose not to watch rewarded ads. Advertising privacy choices do not delete your local game progress.

Website, launch updates and support

If you enter your email in our launch notification form, we store it in Supabase to send Cardlico launch updates. You can ask to be removed by emailing support@cardlico.com. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can respond.

The website uses Vercel hosting and Cloudflare delivery, security and website analytics services. These services process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser and device information, requested pages and performance information, to operate, protect and understand use of the website.

Service providers and sharing

We use Google for sign-in and advertising, Supabase for authentication, leaderboard records and launch signups, and Vercel and Cloudflare for website operation. Information may be processed in countries other than your own. Providers handle information according to their services and privacy terms.

We may also disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. We do not sell our player profiles or launch email list.

Provider policies: Supabase, Vercel, and Cloudflare.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local game data remains on your device until it is reset or removed. Clearing the app's storage removes local data; it does not delete your online account or leaderboard records.

We retain online records, signup information and support correspondence for the purposes described here, subject to deletion requests and any necessary security or legal retention. Service providers may retain their own records under their policies.

You may request access, correction or deletion of information we hold, removal from launch emails, or deletion of your Cardlico online account and associated leaderboard data by emailing support@cardlico.com. For account deletion, contact us from the email used for Google sign-in and include your player display name. We may need to verify your identity. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

Deleting a Cardlico online account does not delete your Google account. We will explain any records that must be retained and why. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or contact a data protection authority.

Children

Cardlico is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact support so we can investigate and address it.

Security and policy changes

We use access controls and encrypted connections for online services, but no system can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy as Cardlico changes. The date above identifies the latest version.
