Delete your Cardlico account

Request removal of your online account and associated data.

Send a deletion request

Email support@cardlico.com with the subject Delete my Cardlico account. Send it from the email you use to sign in to Cardlico with Google. Include your Google Play Games public gamer name, if available. This is the name used for Android rankings, not your local Cardlico Profile name. Your gamer name alone is not proof of account ownership.

We will verify the request and confirm when deletion is complete. If you cannot access that email, contact support for help verifying ownership. Never send passwords or sign-in codes.

What we delete

Your Cardlico Supabase authentication identity, player profile, leaderboard records and associated server data. This does not delete your Google account or Google Play Games profile.

If any information must be retained for legal or security reasons, we will explain what is retained, why and for how long when handling your request.

Data on your device

Your local Cardlico Profile name, progress, scores, settings, achievements and owned items stored locally are separate from your online account. An email request cannot erase data from your phone.

On Android, open Settings, then Apps, then Cardlico, then Storage, and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names vary by device. This removes the local save and cannot be undone through Cardlico. Your local save is not a cloud backup.

Launch emails and other requests

You can also email support to remove your launch signup or request access, correction or deletion of other information we hold. See our privacy policy for details.
