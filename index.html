Three Ways to Play

Campaign, Endless, or Daily

🗺️

Campaign

20 hand-built levels across 5 Acts, each one raising the speed and adding a new twist.

  • Progressive unlock, level by level
  • New special cards and modifiers introduced gradually
  • Coin rewards on every clear
♾️

Endless Mode

No finish line. Speed escalates every 10 cards until you break.

  • Floor speed of 0.6 seconds per card
  • Unlocks once Level 20 is cleared
  • Chase your best run, card by card
📅

Daily Challenge

One shot, once a day, Hearts only — with a speed bonus for staying sharp.

  • Same deck for everyone, every day
  • Global leaderboard, resets daily
  • One attempt — make it count
