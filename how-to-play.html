The Suits

Match the suit to its direction: spades up, hearts down, diamonds left, clubs right.

♠
Spades: swipe up
♥
Hearts: swipe down
♦
Diamonds: swipe left
♣
Clubs: swipe right

Read. Swipe. Repeat.

Swipe in the correct direction before the timer runs out. If a try ends, take what you learned into the next one.

That is all you need to get started. Learn what comes next by playing.
