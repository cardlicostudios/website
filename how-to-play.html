How to Play

Everything you need to know before your first swipe — and everything that'll trip you up after your fiftieth.

The Suits

Every card in the 54-card deck belongs to one of four suits. Each suit has exactly one correct swipe direction. There is no partial credit.

Special Cards

Once you've got the suits down, the deck starts fighting back. Seven special card types can appear, each changing what a correct swipe actually requires.

★
Gold — ×50 score bonus
Swiped correctly, a Gold card pays out fifty times the normal points. It's still bound by suit rules — get greedy and swipe wrong, and it costs you the run just like any other card.
❄️
Frozen — two swipes, one second
A Frozen card needs a second correct swipe within one second of the first to actually clear. Miss the follow-up window and it's game over.
💣
Bomb — 0.8 second fuse
The suit is normal, but the clock isn't. A Bomb card forces a correct swipe inside 0.8 seconds regardless of your current game speed.
🌈
Rainbow — suit hidden
The suit shows as a question mark for 0.8 seconds before revealing itself. Swiping blind is a guess — wait for the reveal, then commit.
👻
Ghost — 50% transparent
The card fades to half opacity. The suit is still technically readable, but at speed it's a genuine squint.
⏱️
Time — +0.5s on correct swipe
A small mercy. Clear a Time card correctly and your countdown gets half a second added back.
🃏
Joker — any direction wins
The one card with no wrong answer. Swipe any direction and it counts as correct.

Modifiers

Modifiers change the rules of an entire level rather than a single card — they stack with special cards and get more aggressive as Acts progress.

🌀
Rotate
Cards oscillate back and forth on screen. Read the suit through the motion — it doesn't stop for you.
🎨
Colour Shift
Suit colours no longer follow the standard black-and-red split, so colour becomes a decoy instead of a shortcut.
🔀
Shuffle Directions
The suit-to-direction mapping rotates partway through the level. What was Up a moment ago might not be Up now.

Campaign Acts

20 levels are organised into 5 Acts of increasing difficulty. Each Act introduces something the last one didn't.

Scoring

Every correct swipe pays a base amount that scales with your current combo, plus any milestone multiplier active in Endless Mode.

🔢
Base points
Each correct swipe earns a flat base score, before any multipliers are applied.
⚡
Combo ×1 – ×10
Every 5 correct swipes in a row raises your combo by one step, up to ×10. A single wrong swipe ends the run — the combo doesn't get a chance to drop first.
🏆
Milestone multipliers
In Endless Mode, crossing a card-count milestone (25 / 50 / 100 / 200) permanently raises your score multiplier for the rest of that run.

Five Tips

01

Read the corner, not just the centre — your eye lands there first once you're fast.

02

On Rainbow cards, don't guess. Losing 0.8 seconds beats losing the run.

03

Chase combo breakpoints in fives — every fifth correct swipe is a multiplier step.

04

In Endless Mode, the floor speed is 0.6s. Practice at that pace in Campaign first.

05

Daily Challenge is Hearts only — it rewards raw reaction speed over suit-reading.
