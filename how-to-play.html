How to Play

Start with the basics. Discover the rest as you play.

The Suits

Match the suit to its direction: spades up, hearts down, diamonds left, clubs right.

♠
↑
Spades → Up
♥
↓
Hearts → Down
♦
←
Diamonds → Left
♣
→
Clubs → Right

Read. Swipe. Repeat.

Swipe in the correct direction before the timer runs out. A wrong swipe or running out of time ends your run.

That is all you need to get started. Learn what comes next by playing.
